Frequently asked questions

Is this video compressor really free?

Yes — completely free, with no signup, no email and no watermark. There’s no paid tier gating the compression.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. Compression runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. Your video file never leaves your device. The engine itself downloads once from a public CDN, then it even works offline.

What video formats are supported?

MP4, MOV and WebM input. The output is an MP4 (H.264 + AAC) that plays virtually everywhere and uploads cleanly to Discord, WhatsApp, email and social platforms.

How small can it make my video?

You set the target. Pick a preset (e.g. Discord 20 MB) or a custom size and the compressor aims for it by lowering the bitrate, and downscaling resolution when needed.

Why is compression slow on big files?

Because your own device does the work — there’s no server farm. Speed depends on your CPU and the clip length. Very large files can hit browser memory limits; try a shorter clip or a larger target if it fails.

Will the quality drop?

Some quality is traded for size — that’s how compression works. Presets balance size and quality; for the best quality at a given size, pick the largest target your destination allows.
